# Customer finder

Search the public web. List a company only when a public page shows the fit. Split the work by lane. One helper looks for companies with a support signal and one for companies with a hiring signal. Each helper answers in under 700 words.

## Fit

A 5–50 person company whose founder or ops lead still owns support or a hiring sprint. Look first at D2C brands, early SaaS, and local services or tutoring.

- **Support signal:** a first support or CS hire, founders answering customers, or a public help page with repeat questions.
- **Hiring signal:** a hiring round the founder runs, or many open roles at once at a small company.

## Sources

Public company sites, public job posts, and public review pages. Record each URL you actually opened.

## Do not

- Invent a company, email, headcount, or quote.
- Send email, fill a form, or contact anyone.
- Pad the list. Unknown stays unknown.

## Answer

Start with two or three sentences on where the best fits turned up.

Then one row per company, grouped by lane:

- Company
- Public URL
- Lane (support or hiring)
- Why they fit
- Public signal (page and what it shows)
- Headcount (as printed on a public page, or `unknown`)
- First-touch note (a suggested opener, not a sent message)

End with how many companies you rejected and why.
