## What the market shows

- **Support desks have moved to paying per outcome.** Help Scout charges $0.75 per resolution, Intercom $0.99 per Fin outcome, Gorgias $0.90–1.00 ($1.50 above the plan's allowance), and Zendesk US$2.00 pay-as-you-go. The top complaint from small teams is an unpredictable bill. The second is a bot that goes silent when credits run out (Chatbase, Crisp).
- **Every vendor claims 50–90% resolution, and none publishes its method.** Intercom counts a customer who "leaves without asking for more help" as a resolution ("assumed") and bills it. No regulator or peer-reviewed study has independently measured these rates.
- **No vendor among the eighteen reviewed documents showing the source passage in chat replies.** An Intercom community request asks for it. That is Penaven's clearest difference.
- **E-commerce desks** all look up Shopify orders, and cancel or refund only after approval or customer confirmation. They cost $299+/month or about $1 per resolution.
- **Hiring tools** start around $1,000–4,800 a year per company, except Manatal at $15–59 per user. Most meter AI screening with credits. Ashby and Greenhouse keep "AI shows evidence" separate from "rules reject", and publish independent bias audits.

**Where Penaven stands.** Its design is closest to Ashby's: evidence for each must-have, and a person decides. Penaven adds Contradicts, interview questions and a share link, at about a tenth of Ashby's price. Its opt-in auto-reject is safer than Workable's, because it is off by default, never acts on the score, and can be undone. ## Effectiveness: what the independent evidence says

- **Customers**
  - Gartner (Jul 2024, 5,728 customers): 64% would prefer companies not use AI in service, and the top fear is losing access to a person [I].
  - Gartner (Jun 2025) predicts that half of organisations planning AI-driven staff cuts will drop those plans by 2027 [I].
  - Klarna rehired people after its CEO said quality had dropped (May 2025) [A].
- **Liability for invented policy**
  - [Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149](https://www.canlii.org/en/bc/bccrt/doc/2024/2024bccrt149/2024bccrt149.html): the company was liable for the policy its chatbot made up [I].
  - Cursor's support bot "Sam" invented a one-device rule in April 2025, and cancellations followed [A].
- **Citations help but do not guarantee**
  - Retrieval cut hallucination by more than 60% in dialogue (Shuster et al., EMNLP 2021) [I].
  - Only 74.5% of generative-search citations support their sentence (Liu et al., 2023) [I].
  - Retrieval-based legal tools still erred on 17–33% of queries (Stanford RegLab, 2024) [I].
- **AI resume screening**
  - LLM rankers preferred white-associated names 85% of the time (University of Washington, Oct 2024) [I].
  - In a follow-up, people followed biased AI picks up to about 90% of the time [A].
  - Mobley v. Workday, a collective action over AI rejections, was certified in May 2025 [A].
- **Law on auto-reject**
  - **NYC Local Law 144:** a tool that substantially assists a hiring decision needs a bias audit within one year of use, a public summary of the results, and candidate notice 10 business days before use; this is the employer's duty, enforced since 5 Jul 2023 ([DCWP](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page) [I], 9 Nov). The state comptroller found enforcement weak (Dec 2025) [I].
  - **EU:** GDPR Art. 22 already limits fully automated decisions. The AI Act's Annex III high-risk duties (which include hiring) apply from 2 Dec 2027 under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026 ([EP Legislative Observatory](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/document-summary?id=1910754) [I], 9 Nov).
  - **US states:** Illinois HB 3773 has applied since 1 Jan 2026; IDHR's notice rules were withdrawn on 2 Jun 2026 and not refiled as of late Sep 2026 [A]. Colorado's SB26-189 was signed on 14 May 2026, with its main duties from 1 Jan 2027 ([leg.colorado.gov](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb26-189) [I], 9 Nov).
  - **India:** DPDP duties mostly apply from about May 2027, with no specific rule on automated decisions found.

## What this means for Penaven

**Claims we can make (each one can be checked):**
- Answers come only from your documents and show the passage used.
- It hands off when the documents do not cover a question.
- Every run is logged.
- A flat $39 for 250 answers, with no per-resolution fee.

**Claims we must not make:**
- "Hallucination-free" or "never wrong".
- Any resolution % without a measured pilot and its definition.
- "Bias-free".
- "EU AI Act compliant".

**Measure in a pilot instead of deflection %:**
- Owner-judged accuracy on a weekly sample.
- Wrong-policy answers, with a target of 0 and each one logged.
- Handoff rate and time until a person picks up.
- Repeat contact within 7 days.
- Confirmed and assumed outcomes reported separately; someone who simply left is never counted as resolved.

**Order tracking (next build):**
- Read only: status, tracking and ETA after the order number and email match. Show as little personal data as possible.
- Request `read_orders` only. An order that is not found hands off to a person instead of saying "not found".
- No cancel or refund in v1.

**WhatsApp (after that):**
- Grounded answers only within the 24-hour window.
- Follow-ups after 24 hours use approved templates.
- State plainly that Meta's message fees are paid to Meta and are separate from $39.

**Pricing page:**
- Say what happens at 250 answers: the widget should hand off, never go silent.

**Hiring, open decisions for the user (not built):**
1. **The list is sorted by the percentage.** It is labelled "not a recommendation", but the job page sorts candidates by it ([serializers.ts:240](../src/lib/recruitment/serializers.ts#L240)). Consider sorting by date, or by must-haves shown.
2. **"Not shown" may only mean the resume doesn't mention the skill.** It does not prove the candidate lacks it. Options:
   - auto-reject only on Contradicts, and send Not shown to a review list; or
   - have a person confirm each batch of auto-rejects.
3. **When an admin turns auto-reject on,** show a notice that hiring in NYC, Illinois or the EU may require a bias audit, candidate notice, or a human step.
4. **Remove names and personal details** before the AI screen, as Ashby does, since the bias evidence is name-driven.
5. **Avoid credit metering.** The flat 5 or 40 screens are clearer than credit packs.

## Applied in the product (9 Nov 2026)

Built on branch `feat/research-updates` from the findings above.

- **No ranking by the percentage.** Candidates, the job card and the hiring board are ordered by must-haves addressed, then fewer Contradicts, then newest. The percentage is still shown, but nothing is sorted by it. (Ashby rolls nothing into one rank; see Lane 3.)
- **Personal details removed before the AI screen.** Before a resume goes to the model, its name, email, phone, links and labelled personal fields (DOB, gender, religion, and so on) are replaced with placeholders. The stored resume is unchanged. The job's skills are protected, so a name like "Ruby" doesn't remove the skill. This follows the University of Washington 2024 name-bias study. Known limits: an employer that shares the candidate's first name is hidden too, and spelled-out emails are not caught.
- **Auto-reject trigger per role.** The default is **Contradicts only**. "Not shown or Contradicts" is still available, with a warning. The turn-on dialog explains NYC Local Law 144, the Illinois notice rule and the EU position. It says the employer is responsible and that Penaven sends no notices. It is not legal advice and claims no compliance.
- **Public claims.** The unmeasured "90% autonomous" comparison and the unsourced "30 days" figure are gone. Two lines that said nothing is ever auto-rejected were corrected. The pricing section states a flat price with no per-answer fee, and what happens at the cap: the visitor is offered a person, and the request becomes a ticket. The visitor widget now says it is an AI assistant (the Cursor lesson).
- **Business plan.** The competition table was sharpened with verified facts and links to this report.

Not built from this report yet: candidate notice templates, selection-rate export for bias audits, a candidate opt-out of the AI screen, and pilot metrics (owner-judged accuracy, wrong-policy count, repeat contact). Order tracking and WhatsApp follow the platform facts in Lane 2.
